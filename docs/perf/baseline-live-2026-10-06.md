> **Read before using these numbers**
>
> 1. **The "Outcome" column is wrong in this run.** The script bug is fixed in `scripts/baseline.py` (`_outcome`). A
>    ready trip also carries a feasibility record, which was misread as "infeasible". From the stage traces:
>    - Plans that reached `stage.itinerary` produced an itinerary.
>    - Udaipur → Rishikesh and New Delhi → Istanbul stopped after the routes stage, so they were refused.
>    - A profiled re-run of Jaipur → New Delhi ended `ready`.
> 2. **Wall times here are dominated by SQLite lock waits, not by providers or CPU.** A cProfile of one plan
>    (`scripts/profile_plan.py`) put 194 of 215 s in `sqlite3.Cursor.execute`:
>    - The planner's session holds a write transaction from `upsert_places` (planner.py:226) until the end of the plan.
>    - Every response-cache write from another session waits out `busy_timeout=30000`, then fails silently.
>    - That is also why the "warm" pass was barely warm: those cache writes never landed.
>
>    PostgreSQL would not block this way, so these times are not representative of production. They are what a
>    developer on SQLite sees today.
